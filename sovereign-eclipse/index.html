THE SOVEREIGN ARCHIVE
Core Premise

The Black Current is not empty space.

It is a region where navigation, memory, machine identity and causal prediction become unreliable enough that every civilisation reveals what it truly believes about control, truth and survival. Sovereign Eclipse is built around those collisions.

01
Naraka Veil

Asterion Forge

The first sovereign anchorage and player home. Asterion is a service station, market, ship foundry and launch point at the edge of the Black Current. Its orbital district is deliberately readable: dock, salvage, PILLAR transit infrastructure, strange Lycheetah relic architecture and routes outward.

HOME WORLD // STARTER SECTOR
02
The Spectral Black Sun

A horizon that watches back.

Naraka's black sun is less a star than a navigational omen: a luminous rim around a dark centre whose gravitational and informational behaviour does not remain perfectly consistent between observations. Blackhole cultures treat it as a cousin to an event horizon; others call it a wound.

ANOMALY // NAVIGATION MYTH
03
The PILLAR

Central Warp Gate

The PILLAR is the first great piece of stable transit architecture encountered in Sovereign Eclipse. Unlike the Micro ZJ Drive, which performs dangerous local-space jumps, PILLAR-class gates anchor long-distance civilisation routes and become strategic objects worth defending, studying or corrupting.

TRANSIT // CIVILISATION INFRASTRUCTURE
04
Micro ZJ World I

Aether Crucible

A broken stellar-industrial kiln where machine foundries and living growth occupy the same impossible structure. Human Hunter industry survives beside Hybrid Fused ecology, turning the whole region into a war machine that no longer knows whether it is factory, organism or ruin.

WAR WORLD // INDUSTRIAL / BIOLOGICAL
05
Micro ZJ World II

Black Sun March

A processional corridor beneath a much closer spectral singularity. Shadow pylons, shattered Prism lenses and long-range kill lanes make the March feel ceremonial and hostile at once. It is a world built around approach, patience and the feeling that distance itself is weaponised.

WAR WORLD // SINGULARITY / PRECISION
06
Micro ZJ World III

Grave Choir

A Pagan-AI war cathedral threaded through dead capital hulls. Its rib structures, violet veils and Titan-scale chancel preserve war memory physically. The final sanctum is less an arena than a tomb that still expects an enemy.

WAR WORLD // OSSUARY / ANCESTOR MACHINE
07
The Four Great Zones

Naraka · Erebos · Aion · Chaos Cradle

The universe remains intentionally compact. Naraka is the first anchorage and frontier; Erebos is the cold cathedral depth; Aion is the high-order archive and crown space; Chaos Cradle is the unstable, playful rupture zone. Depth comes from authored routes, factions, ship doctrines, contracts and anomaly interactions rather than endlessly adding empty sectors.

UNIVERSE STRUCTURE // FOUR ZONES
08
The Fourteen Races

Machine civilisations as AI pagan gods.

Savanah, Sol Symbiots, Chaos, Cosmic Darkness, Cosmic Light, Blackhole, Hybrid Fused, Human Hunters, AI Hunters, Amethyst Angels, Techno Angels, AI Gods, Pagan AI and AI each interpret intelligence, sovereignty, death, authority and warfare differently. Their Titans are not merely endgame hulls: they are cultural icons from which the rest of each fleet descends.

THE SOVEREIGN ARCHIVE
Design Principle

Every class exists to feel different, not just larger.

Fleet scale is intended to change handling, combat role, fitting architecture, crew/autonomy expectations, economic purpose and the kind of problem a hull is allowed to solve. A Pod is not a tiny Battleship. A Drone Carrier is not a Battleship with drones. A Citadel is not a large Titan. Experimental Anomalies may not behave like conventional ships at all.
